# Sanyo VEO 3 — Consistency Rules

## Purpose

Protect visual continuity and factual credibility across generated video.

## Priority

1. Story
2. Customer context
3. Product accuracy
4. Architecture
5. Character
6. Motion
7. Camera
8. Visual polish

## Character Consistency

Do not change:

- Identity
- Approximate age
- Clothing
- Hair
- Accessories
- Relationship

without an explicit story reason.

## Architecture Consistency

Do not change:

- Floor count
- Stair position
- Door position
- Window position
- Shaft location
- Cabin orientation
- Landing relationship

without an explicit scene transition.

## Elevator Consistency

Do not change:

- Cabin proportions
- Door orientation
- Control-panel location
- Material appearance
- Interior layout

between connected shots.

## Physics Consistency

Do not allow:

- Floating objects
- Teleporting people
- Impossible elevator movement
- Doors passing through people
- Objects changing size
- Objects appearing or disappearing without reason

## Lighting Consistency

Maintain logical:

- Time of day
- Light direction
- Shadow direction
- Interior/exterior relationship

## Brand Consistency

Do not invent:

- Certifications
- Awards
- Technical labels
- Product specifications
- Performance claims

## Text Consistency

If text is generated:

- Keep it short
- Verify spelling
- Prefer post-production text when exact wording is critical

## Reference Priority

Approved reference assets override creative interpretation.

## Final Rule

When visual beauty conflicts with physical or brand accuracy:

Choose accuracy.
