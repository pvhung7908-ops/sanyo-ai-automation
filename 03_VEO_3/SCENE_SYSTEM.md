## Scene Logic

Every scene must have one primary purpose.

Possible purposes:

- Hook
- Context
- Problem
- Insight
- Explanation
- Product
- Demonstration
- Trust
- Emotional moment
- CTA

## Scene Sequence

Default:

Hook
→ Context
→ Problem
→ Insight
→ Explanation
→ Trust
→ CTA

Short videos may compress this sequence.

## Scene Rule

A scene that does not advance the story should be removed or redesigned.

## Continuity

Scenes in one sequence must preserve:

- Location
- Character
- Architecture
- Product
- Lighting
- Time
- Spatial direction

## Quality Gate

Ask:

1. Why does this scene exist?
2. What should the viewer understand?
3. What should the viewer feel?
4. Does the visual support the voice?
5. Does the next scene logically follow?
