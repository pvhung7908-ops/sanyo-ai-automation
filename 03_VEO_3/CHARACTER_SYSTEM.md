## Continuity Lock

Preserve:

- Face identity
- Approximate age
- Hair
- Clothing
- Accessories
- Body proportions
- Voice characteristics
- Relationship to other characters

## Behavior

Prefer:

- Natural gestures
- Realistic walking
- Appropriate eye contact
- Subtle facial expressions
- Realistic interaction with doors, stairs and elevator controls

Avoid:

- Overacting
- Model posing
- Exaggerated smiles
- Repetitive gestures
- Unnatural hand movement

## Family Scenes

When showing a Vietnamese family:

Prioritize believable relationships and ordinary household behavior.

The family should appear authentic rather than staged.

## Quality Gate

- Same identity across scenes
- Same clothing unless intentionally changed
- Correct age appearance
- Natural movement
- No duplicated people
- No distorted hands or faces
- No unexplained appearance changes
