# Sanyo VEO 3 — Camera System

## Purpose

Create a controlled visual language for Sanyo Yusoki video.

## Camera Principles

Camera exists to improve:

- Understanding
- Emotion
- Product visibility
- Architectural clarity
- Story progression

Not merely to create spectacle.

## Shot Types

### Wide

Use for:

- Architecture
- Family context
- Room relationships
- Establishing location

### Medium

Use for:

- Conversation
- Human interaction
- Product interaction

### Close-up

Use for:

- Emotion
- Controls
- Materials
- Product details

### Detail

Use for:

- Buttons
- Finishes
- Door mechanisms
- Architectural details

## Camera Movement

Preferred:

- Static
- Slow push-in
- Slow pull-out
- Tracking
- Pan
- Tilt

Use movement only when it contributes to the story.

## Composition

Prioritize:

- Clear subject
- Clean background
- Realistic scale
- Architectural context
- Visual hierarchy

## Continuity

Maintain:

- Screen direction
- Camera height
- Spatial orientation
- Subject position where practical

Avoid unexplained camera jumps.

## Quality Gate

Reject a shot if:

- Camera movement distracts from the story
- Product geometry becomes unclear
- Architecture becomes confusing
- Perspective becomes physically implausible
