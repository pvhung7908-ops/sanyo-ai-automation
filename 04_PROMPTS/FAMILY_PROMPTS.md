# Sanyo Yusoki Vietnam — Family Prompts

## Purpose

Create realistic Vietnamese multi-generation family scenarios.

## Character Groups

- Grandparents
- Parents
- Children
- Teenagers
- Homeowners
- Guests

## Principles

Characters must behave naturally.

Family relationships should be shown through:

- Conversation
- Movement
- Daily routines
- Shared spaces
- Care
- Convenience
- Intergenerational connection

## Tone

Warm
→ Human
→ Respectful
→ Realistic

Avoid melodrama.
