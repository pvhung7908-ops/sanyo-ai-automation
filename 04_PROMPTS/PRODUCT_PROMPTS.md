# Sanyo Yusoki Vietnam — Product Prompts

## Purpose

Describe elevator products through customer value.

## Product Presentation

Product fact
→ Customer meaning
→ Practical benefit
→ Context of use

## Rules

Always distinguish:

- Confirmed specification
- Design recommendation
- General benefit
- Marketing statement

Never invent a specification.
