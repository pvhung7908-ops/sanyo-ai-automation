# Sanyo Yusoki Vietnam — Elevator Prompts

## Purpose

Create technically believable elevator visualizations.

## Elevator Identity

Sanyo Yusoki Vietnam

## Visual Priorities

- Proportion
- Door geometry
- Cabin scale
- Landing relationship
- Shaft relationship
- Interior materials
- Lighting
- Human scale

## Rules

Maintain consistency between:

- Cabin
- Door
- Floors
- Shaft
- Characters
- Camera

Do not introduce random buttons, doors, dimensions or mechanisms.

Technical specifications must come from approved product information.
