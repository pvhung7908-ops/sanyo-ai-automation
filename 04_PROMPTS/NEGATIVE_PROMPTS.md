# Sanyo Yusoki Vietnam — Negative Prompts

## Purpose

Prevent visual, brand and storytelling errors.

## General Negative Constraints

Avoid:

- Distorted human anatomy
- Extra fingers
- Unnatural movement
- Floating objects
- Impossible architecture
- Inconsistent elevator geometry
- Random text
- Random logos
- Incorrect brand identity
- Unstable character appearance
- Changing clothing between shots
- Changing cabin proportions
- Impossible camera movement
- Excessive cinematic effects
- Fear-based advertising
- Aggressive sales language
- Unsupported technical claims

## Consistency

Characters, locations, elevator design and materials must remain stable throughout the sequence.

## Brand Safety

Do not create claims that cannot be supported by approved Sanyo Yusoki information.
