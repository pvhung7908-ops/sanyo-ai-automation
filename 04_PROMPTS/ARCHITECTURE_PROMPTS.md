# Sanyo Yusoki Vietnam — Architecture Prompts

## Purpose

Represent Vietnamese residential architecture accurately.

## Main Contexts

- Narrow tube houses
- Small urban houses
- Multi-floor homes
- Renovated houses
- Houses approximately 35–60 m2
- Multi-generation residences

## Visual Principles

Show the relationship between:

- Floor area
- Vertical circulation
- Staircase
- Elevator
- Natural light
- Interior circulation
- Family living areas

## Rules

Architecture must remain physically believable.

Do not create impossible structural layouts merely for visual effect.
