# Sanyo Yusoki Vietnam — Story Prompts

## Purpose

Turn customer problems into short, emotionally understandable stories.

## Story Flow

Problem
→ Insight
→ Human situation
→ Better possibility
→ Solution
→ Trust
→ Action

## Story Principles

The story should feel like:

- A real family situation
- A useful observation
- An expert helping the customer think
- A solution discovered naturally

## Avoid

- Hard selling
- Empty slogans
- Unrealistic family behavior
- Unsupported technical claims
- Repetitive product descriptions
