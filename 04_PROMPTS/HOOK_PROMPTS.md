# Sanyo Yusoki Vietnam — Hook Prompts

## Purpose

Generate opening moments that immediately establish customer relevance.

## Hook Principles

A hook should create at least one of:

- Recognition
- Curiosity
- Tension
- Surprise
- A useful question
- A relatable family situation

## Hook Structure

Customer situation
→ Unexpected observation
→ Open question or insight

## Rules

- Do not exaggerate technical claims.
- Do not use fear unnecessarily.
- Do not attack competitors.
- Do not begin with generic advertising language.
- Prefer real Vietnamese family situations.

## Examples of Direction

- A family renovating an old narrow house discovers a hidden space problem.
- Parents and children discuss how the house should work for several generations.
- An architect notices that circulation has become the biggest limitation of a small house.
