# Sanyo Yusoki Vietnam — CTA Prompts

## Purpose

Create natural calls to action appropriate to the customer's stage.

## CTA Principles

CTA should be:

- Natural
- Helpful
- Relevant
- Low pressure
- Never aggressive

## Customer Stages

### Awareness

Invite the customer to learn more.

### Consideration

Invite comparison, consultation or planning.

### Decision

Invite a concrete consultation or site discussion.

### Existing Customer

Invite support, maintenance or service contact.

## Rule

The CTA should feel like the next useful step,
not a forced sales request.
