# Sanyo Yusoki Vietnam — Architecture Assets

## Purpose

Provide realistic Vietnamese residential architecture references.

## Main Environments

- Narrow tube house
- Small urban house
- Multi-floor family house
- Renovated house
- Contemporary villa
- Small footprint residence
- Multi-generation residence

## Reference Elements

- Facade
- Staircase
- Elevator shaft
- Living room
- Kitchen
- Bedroom
- Landing
- Roof
- Courtyard
- Natural light

## Rules

Architecture must remain physically believable.

The elevator must have a plausible relationship with:

- Floor structure
- Staircase
- Landing
- Door
- Shaft
- Human circulation

## Naming Convention

ARCH_[TYPE]_[STYLE]_[VIEW]_V[VERSION]
