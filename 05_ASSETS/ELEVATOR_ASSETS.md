# Sanyo Yusoki Vietnam — Elevator Assets

## Purpose

Centralize approved elevator product references.

## Asset Types

- Complete elevator
- Landing door
- Control panel
- Door operator
- Guide components
- Machine room / machine-space references
- Safety components
- Product photography
- Product renderings

## Rules

Use approved references whenever product appearance matters.

Do not invent product specifications.

Do not alter critical product geometry.

## Naming Convention

ELEVATOR_[MODEL]_[TYPE]_V[VERSION]

Example:

ELEVATOR_SMART_FULL_V01
