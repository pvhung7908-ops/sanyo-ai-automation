# Sanyo Yusoki Vietnam — Brand Assets

## Purpose

Define approved visual assets representing the Sanyo Yusoki Vietnam brand.

## Asset Types

- Logo
- Wordmark
- Brand typography
- Brand colors
- Brand symbols
- Showroom identity
- Approved brand graphics

## Rules

Brand assets must not be distorted, recolored or modified without approval.

Do not invent alternative logos.

Do not generate fake certification marks.

Do not combine the Sanyo Yusoki identity with unrelated brands.

## Naming Convention

BRAND_[TYPE]_[NAME]_V[VERSION]

Example:

BRAND_LOGO_PRIMARY_V01
