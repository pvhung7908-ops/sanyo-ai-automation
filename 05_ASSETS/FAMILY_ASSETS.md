# Sanyo Yusoki Vietnam — Family Assets

## Purpose

Create consistent family environments for storytelling.

## Character Groups

- Grandparents
- Parents
- Teenagers
- Children
- Guests

## Story Contexts

- Morning routine
- Returning home
- Family gathering
- Going upstairs
- Receiving guests
- Renovating the house
- Discussing family needs
- Using the elevator

## Principles

Characters should feel like a realistic Vietnamese family.

Avoid stereotypes.

Avoid exaggerated emotional behavior.

## Naming Convention

FAMILY_[GROUP]_[SCENE]_V[VERSION]

Example:

FAMILY_MULTIGEN_RETURN_HOME_V01
