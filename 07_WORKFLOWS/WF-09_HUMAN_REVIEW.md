# WF-09 — Human Review

## Purpose

Provide human approval where trust, safety or technical accuracy requires it.

## Mandatory Review

Technical specifications
Safety claims
Legal claims
Pricing
Performance guarantees
Testimonials
Regulatory statements
Sensitive customer situations

## Input

content_id
production_id
quality_score
review_notes

## Decision

APPROVE
REJECT
REVISION

## APPROVE

→ WF-10

## REVISION

→ WF-03
or
→ WF-05
or
→ WF-07

## REJECT

→ ARCHIVED

## Audit

Record:

reviewer
decision
notes
timestamp
version
