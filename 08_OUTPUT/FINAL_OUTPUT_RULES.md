# Final Output Rules

## FINAL is not RAW

A generated video is not automatically a final video.

Required:

1. Quality Gate
2. Human Review
3. Calibration
4. Final validation

---

## Final Validation

Check:

Content ID
Version
Brand
Product
Technical information
Visual continuity
Audio
CTA
Legal/safety
Output format

---

## Final Naming

CONTENT_ID_VERSION_FINAL

Example:

SYV-2026-000001_V01_FINAL

---

## Archive

Previous versions remain archived.

Never delete a previous approved version merely because a newer version exists.
