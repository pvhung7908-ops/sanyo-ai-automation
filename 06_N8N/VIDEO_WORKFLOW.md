# WF-04 — Video Brief Workflow

## Purpose

Transform approved content into a production-ready video brief.

---

## Input

content_id
customer_insight
content_angle
platform
duration
cta

---

## Output

brief_id
hook
story
scene_structure
camera_direction
character_direction
visual_direction
audio_direction
cta

---

## Standard 60 Second Structure

0–5 sec
HOOK

5–25 sec
STORY

25–50 sec
ANALYSIS / EXPLANATION

50–60 sec
CTA

---

## Production Rule

The Video Brief must be understandable without seeing the original content.

Every scene must have a clear purpose.
