# WF-09 — Human Review

## Human approval required for

- Technical specifications
- Safety claims
- Legal claims
- Pricing
- Performance guarantees
- Testimonials
- Regulatory statements
- Sensitive customer situations

---

## Review Decision

APPROVE
REJECT
REVISION

decision
notes
timestamp
reviewer

## Principle

Automation accelerates production.
Human review protects trust.
