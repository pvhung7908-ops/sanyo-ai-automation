## 5. Customer Stage

Every video should identify the customer's stage.

### Stage 1 — Unaware

Customer does not yet recognize the problem.

Content objective:
- Create awareness
- Create curiosity
- Reveal hidden problems

Typical approach:
- Observation
- Contrarian insight
- Unexpected question
- Everyday situation

CTA:
- Low friction
- Continue learning
- Save
- Follow

---

### Stage 2 — Problem Aware

Customer recognizes the problem but does not know the best solution.

Content objective:
- Clarify the problem
- Explain consequences
- Help customer think differently

Typical approach:
- Problem analysis
- Mistakes
- Checklist
- Before/after thinking

CTA:
- Ask a question
- Request information
- See more examples

---

### Stage 3 — Solution Aware

Customer is considering possible solutions.

Content objective:
- Explain solution logic
- Compare approaches fairly
- Reduce uncertainty

Typical approach:
- Expert explanation
- Case study
- Design logic
- Technical explanation

CTA:
- Discuss the project
- Request consultation
- Review options

---

### Stage 4 — Brand Aware

Customer knows Sanyo Yusoki and is evaluating trust.

Content objective:
- Demonstrate credibility
- Explain process
- Show service capability
- Reduce purchase risk

Typical approach:
- Behind the scenes
- Installation
- Maintenance
- Engineering
- Customer experience

CTA:
- Contact
- Visit showroom
- Discuss requirements

---

### Stage 5 — Decision Ready

Customer is close to making a decision.

Content objective:
- Remove final friction
- Clarify next step
- Provide useful decision information

Typical approach:
- Project checklist
- Questions to ask suppliers
- Specification explanation
- Installation preparation
- Service explanation

CTA:
- Book consultation
- Send project information
- Request site assessment

---

## 6. Video Objective

Each brief must select one primary objective.

Allowed primary objectives:

- Awareness
- Education
- Trust
- Consideration
- Lead generation
- Customer reassurance
- Brand authority
- Product explanation
- Service explanation
- Retention

Do not use multiple primary objectives.

Secondary objectives may be added only when necessary.

## 7. Audience Definition

The brief must identify:

### Primary Audience

Who is the video mainly for?

Examples:

- Homeowner
- Multi-generation family
- Home renovation customer
- Architect
- Contractor
- Property investor
- Hospital/project owner
- Hotel/project owner

### Audience Situation

Describe what is happening in the customer's life or project.

Examples:

- Renovating an old narrow house
- Building a new townhouse
- Adding an elevator to an existing home
- Choosing between suppliers
- Concerned about maintenance
- Concerned about space
- Concerned about safety
- Concerned about long-term reliability

### Audience Question

Write the question the customer is actually asking.

Example:

"Nhà tôi nhỏ như vậy thì đặt thang máy ở đâu?"

---

## 8. Customer Insight

The brief must distinguish:

Problem
from
Insight.

Problem:
What the customer says.

Insight:
Why the problem matters emotionally or practically.

Example:

Problem:
"Nhà tôi không đủ diện tích để làm thang máy."

Insight:
"Gia đình muốn có thang máy để ông bà và trẻ nhỏ đi lại thuận tiện hơn, nhưng sợ phải hy sinh quá nhiều không gian sống."

Insight must be human, specific and useful for storytelling.

---

## 9. Big Idea

The Big Idea is one sentence that expresses the central thought of the video.

It must be:

- Simple
- Relevant
- Memorable
- Customer-centered
- Consistent with the brand
- Capable of generating visual storytelling

Avoid generic ideas such as:

"Thang máy tốt cho cuộc sống."

Prefer a concrete customer truth.

Example:

"Nhà nhỏ không có nghĩa là gia đình phải sống bất tiện."

---

## 10. Hook

The first seconds must earn attention.

A Hook may be:

- Question
- Contrarian statement
- Surprising fact
- Customer situation
- Visual contradiction
- Common mistake
- Emotional observation

Hook rules:

- Customer first
- Short
- Natural
- Specific
- No exaggerated claims
- No fake urgency
- No unsupported statistics

Avoid:

"Bạn sẽ không tin điều này!"

Prefer:

"Nhà 40m² có làm được thang máy không?"

---

## 11. Hook Quality Test

A good hook should answer at least one:

- Why should I care?
- Is this my problem?
- Is there something I do not know?
- Could this save me a mistake?
- Does this challenge my assumption?

If the answer is no, rewrite the hook.

---

## 12. Story Structure

Default structure:

Hook
→ Context
→ Problem
→ Insight
→ Explanation
→ Solution logic
→ Trust
→ CTA

For short-form video, compress the structure rather than removing the customer logic.

---

## 13. Standard 60-Second Structure

Default timing:

### 0–5 seconds
HOOK

Purpose:
Stop scrolling and identify the customer problem.

### 5–15 seconds
CONTEXT

Show the real-life situation.

### 15–30 seconds
PROBLEM + INSIGHT

Explain why the problem exists and why it matters.

### 30–45 seconds
SOLUTION / EXPERT EXPLANATION

Provide useful reasoning.

### 45–55 seconds
TRUST / PROOF

Use:
- Process
- Experience
- Technical detail
- Project context
- Service explanation
- Real asset

Do not manufacture proof.

### 55–60 seconds
CTA

One natural next step.

---

## 14. Short Video Structure

For 15–30 seconds:

Hook
→ Problem
→ One Insight
→ One Useful Answer
→ CTA

Do not attempt to explain everything.

---

## 15. Long Video Structure

For 90–180 seconds:

Hook
→ Situation
→ Problem
→ Why common solutions fail
→ Insight
→ Solution framework
→ Example
→ Trust
→ CTA

Long-form videos must still maintain one central idea.

---

## 16. Story Principle

The customer is the protagonist.

Sanyo is the guide.

Do not make the brand the hero of every story.

Preferred structure:

Customer has a problem
→ Customer discovers a better way to think
→ Sanyo provides expertise
→ Customer moves toward a better decision

## 19. Camera Direction

Use camera movement only when it supports the story.

Allowed directions:

- Static
- Slow push-in
- Slow pull-out
- Pan
- Tracking
- Close-up
- Wide establishing shot
- Over-the-shoulder
- Top view
- Detail shot

Avoid excessive cinematic movement.

The camera should serve clarity.

---

## 20. Visual Realism

For product and architectural videos:

- Maintain realistic proportions
- Maintain architectural continuity
- Maintain elevator dimensions
- Maintain door positions
- Maintain floor relationships
- Maintain cabin orientation
- Avoid impossible construction
- Avoid physically impossible elevator movement

When exact technical information is unavailable, do not invent it.

---

## 21. Voice

Voice style should follow the brand profile.

Default characteristics:

- Calm
- Intelligent
- Trustworthy
- Helpful
- Human
- Expert without arrogance

Avoid:

- Shouting
- Excessive hype
- Fake urgency
- Overly promotional language
- Unsupported superlatives

---

## 22. Dialogue

Dialogue should sound like real Vietnamese conversation.

Prefer:

"Nhà anh chị hiện tại rộng bao nhiêu mét?"

over:

"Quý khách có nhu cầu sử dụng giải pháp thang máy tối ưu diện tích hay không?"

Natural language increases trust.

---

## 23. On-Screen Text

On-screen text must:

- Be short
- Be readable
- Support the message
- Not duplicate every spoken sentence

Use important phrases.

Examples:

"NHÀ 40m² CÓ LÀM ĐƯỢC THANG MÁY?"

"ĐỪNG CHỌN THANG MÁY TRƯỚC KHI XEM MẶT BẰNG"

"3 ĐIỂM CẦN KIỂM TRA"

---

## 24. CTA Principle

CTA must be:

- Natural
- Helpful
- Relevant to the customer's stage
- Never aggressive

CTA should continue the customer's decision journey.

Examples:

Unaware:
"Bạn có từng gặp tình huống này?"

Problem Aware:
"Lưu lại để kiểm tra khi cải tạo nhà."

Solution Aware:
"Nếu bạn đang có mặt bằng tương tự, hãy gửi kích thước để được phân tích."

Brand Aware:
"Có thể xem trực tiếp tại showroom."

Decision Ready:
"Gửi mặt bằng để trao đổi phương án."

---

## 25. CTA Restrictions

Never use:

- Fake scarcity
- False urgency
- Unsupported discounts
- Guaranteed outcomes
- Misleading claims

Do not pressure the customer.

---

## 26. Brand Integration

Brand presence should be proportional to the story.

Use brand elements when they increase:

- Trust
- Recognition
- Understanding
- Proof

Do not force logos or slogans into every scene.

---

## 27. Product Claims

Every factual product claim must be traceable to an approved source.

Potential sources:

- Approved product documentation
- Technical documentation
- Company-approved information
- Approved project data
- Approved sales material

If a claim cannot be verified:

Do not present it as fact.

Use:

"có thể"
"tùy phương án"
"cần khảo sát"
"cần kiểm tra thực tế"

when appropriate.

---

## 28. Technical Content

For technical videos, distinguish:

Fact
→ Explanation
→ Recommendation

Example:

Fact:
"The available shaft dimension is X."

Explanation:
"This affects cabin and structural options."

Recommendation:
"Final dimensions should be confirmed after site survey."

Never convert assumptions into specifications.

---

## 29. Emotional Content

Emotional storytelling may use:

- Family
- Convenience
- Aging parents
- Children
- Daily movement
- Renovation anxiety
- Long-term peace of mind

The emotion must remain authentic.

Do not exploit fear.

---

Before approving a brief, ask:

"Why would this customer care today?"

If the answer is unclear, revise the idea.

## 35. Quality Gate

Before production, check:

### Accuracy

- Are factual claims supported?
- Are technical claims verified?
- Are dimensions realistic?
- Are product statements approved?

### Brand Consistency

- Does the video fit the brand?
- Is the tone trustworthy?
- Is the brand represented accurately?

### Customer Relevance

- Is the customer problem real?
- Is the insight specific?
- Does the video answer a meaningful question?

### Clarity

- Is there one central idea?
- Is the hook understandable?
- Is the story easy to follow?

### Trustworthiness

- Is proof real?
- Are claims appropriately qualified?
- Are assumptions separated from facts?

### Legal / Safety

- No misleading claims
- No unsafe instructions
- No fabricated certification
- No fabricated technical performance
- No fear-based manipulation

### CTA

- Natural
- Helpful
- Relevant
- Not aggressive

A brief should not proceed if a critical quality gate fails.

## 41. Example

Customer Problem:

"Nhà phố nhỏ, muốn có thang máy nhưng sợ mất diện tích."

Customer Insight:

"Gia đình muốn cha mẹ lớn tuổi và trẻ nhỏ đi lại thuận tiện hơn nhưng không muốn biến ngôi nhà thành một công trình chỉ để dành chỗ cho thang máy."

Big Idea:

"Thiết kế thang máy đúng bắt đầu từ việc hiểu ngôi nhà."

Hook:

"Nhà 40m² có nhất thiết phải bỏ ý định làm thang máy?"

Story:

Show the narrow house.
Show the staircase.
Introduce the family's concern.
Explain that elevator planning should begin with the existing layout.
Show design considerations.
End with a practical next step.

CTA:

"Nếu bạn đang cải tạo nhà phố nhỏ, hãy gửi mặt bằng để cùng xem phương án."

## 42. Storytelling Rule

Do not begin with:

"We are Sanyo Yusoki..."

unless the objective specifically requires a brand introduction.

Begin with the customer's world.

The brand enters when it becomes useful.

---

## 43. Content Philosophy

Useful before promotional.

Insight before product.

Trust before conversion.

Customer before brand.

Clarity before complexity.

Evidence before claims.

Long-term relationship before short-term pressure.

---

## 44. Master Rule

Every video must answer:

Who is this for?

What problem are they experiencing?

What insight do they need?

What should they understand after watching?

What should they feel?

What should they do next?

If these questions cannot be answered clearly, the brief is not ready.
