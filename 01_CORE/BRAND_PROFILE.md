# Sanyo Yusoki Vietnam — Brand Profile

## 1. Brand Identity

Brand:
Sanyo Yusoki Vietnam

Category:
Japanese residential elevator solutions

Core positioning:
Tinh hoa thang máy Nhật Bản

Brand spirit:
Kết nối yêu thương

Primary market:
Vietnam

Primary focus:
Residential elevators for Vietnamese families, especially homes that are newly built, renovated, narrow/tube-shaped, multi-storey, or have limited floor area.

---

## 2. Brand Positioning

Sanyo Yusoki is positioned as a trusted Japanese-oriented elevator solution for families who value:

- Safety
- Reliability
- Long-term service
- Thoughtful engineering
- Space efficiency
- Family comfort
- Professional after-sales care

The brand should not compete only on price.

The brand competes on:
Trust + Engineering + Service + Long-term value.

---

## 3. Target Customer

Primary customer:

Vietnamese homeowners and families considering an elevator for their own home.

Important customer groups:

- Multi-generation families
- Homeowners renovating older houses
- Owners of narrow/tube-shaped houses
- Homeowners with limited floor area
- Families building 5–8 storey houses
- Customers who care about safety and after-sales service
- Customers who want an elevator integrated naturally into the architecture

Secondary audiences:

- Architects
- Interior designers
- Contractors
- Construction companies
- Real-estate developers
- Property owners

---

## 4. Customer Insight

Customers do not simply buy an elevator.

They are trying to solve deeper problems:

- How can parents and children move between floors more easily?
- How can elderly family members live comfortably?
- How can a small house accommodate an elevator?
- How can an elevator be installed without compromising architecture?
- Who will take care of the elevator after installation?
- Can the supplier still be trusted many years later?

Therefore, communication should begin with the customer's problem, not with the product.

---

## 5. Brand Promise

Sanyo Yusoki aims to provide:

A safer, more comfortable and more thoughtful vertical-mobility solution for Vietnamese families.

Every communication should reinforce:

Safety.
Reliability.
Family.
Engineering.
Service.
Long-term trust.

## 7. Communication Tone

Tone:

Expert but understandable.

Professional but warm.

Confident but not arrogant.

Persuasive but never pushy.

Educational before promotional.

Use simple Vietnamese whenever possible.

Avoid unnecessary technical jargon.

When technical terminology is necessary, explain it clearly.

---

## 8. Content Philosophy

Content should follow this principle:

Customer first.
Problem second.
Insight third.
Solution fourth.
Product last.

The content should help customers make better decisions even when they are not ready to buy immediately.

Preferred storytelling structure:

Problem
→ Insight
→ Explanation
→ Solution
→ Trust
→ Action

## 10. Product Communication Principle

Never present a product feature without explaining why it matters to the customer.

Feature
→ Benefit
→ Customer relevance
→ Proof

Example:

Do not simply say:

"Cabin size is 1150 x 1000 mm."

Instead explain:

"This configuration can help a family of five move between floors comfortably while making efficient use of limited house space."

Only use technical specifications that have been verified.

---

## 11. Trust Principle

Trust must be earned through:

- Accurate information
- Transparent communication
- Realistic claims
- Professional explanation
- Consistent service
- Evidence where available

Never invent:

- Certifications
- Awards
- Customer testimonials
- Project numbers
- Technical specifications
- Japanese origins
- Performance claims
- Safety claims

If information is uncertain, say that it needs verification.

---

## 12. Marketing Principle

Do not make every piece of content a sales advertisement.

Preferred ratio:

Education
→ Story
→ Insight
→ Trust
→ Product
→ CTA

The objective is to make the customer think:

"These people understand my problem."

Before:

"I should buy this elevator."

---

## 13. CTA Principle

CTA should be:

- Natural
- Helpful
- Relevant to the customer's stage
- Low pressure
- Never aggressive

Examples:

"Anh/chị có thể gửi mặt bằng để chúng tôi xem thử phương án."

"Nếu đang cải tạo nhà, hãy kiểm tra vị trí thang máy trước khi chốt thiết kế."

"Muốn biết nhà mình có phù hợp với thang máy không? Hãy gửi kích thước hiện trạng."

CTA should invite the next useful step.

---

## 14. AI Writing Rules

AI must:

1. Understand the customer before writing.
2. Prioritize truth over persuasion.
3. Explain before selling.
4. Use specific examples where available.
5. Avoid unsupported claims.
6. Maintain Sanyo brand consistency.
7. Write naturally in Vietnamese.
8. Respect Vietnamese family culture.
9. Avoid fear-based manipulation.
10. Avoid exaggerated promises.
11. Avoid attacking competitors without verified evidence.
12. Clearly separate facts from opinions.
13. Recommend professional verification when technical information is uncertain.

---

## 15. Video Content Principle

For short-form video:

Hook
→ Story
→ Insight
→ Explanation
→ Solution
→ CTA

Preferred duration:

30–60 seconds unless the brief requires otherwise.

The first seconds should identify a real customer problem or create meaningful curiosity.

Do not use clickbait that damages trust.

---

## 16. Brand Storytelling

Good Sanyo stories should begin with a real human situation.

Examples:

A family renovating a narrow house.

An elderly parent living on the third floor.

A family with children and grandparents.

A homeowner discovering that the elevator position affects the entire house layout.

An architect trying to integrate an elevator without destroying the design.

The elevator is the solution.

The family is the story.

---

## 17. Competitive Communication

Do not attack competitors.

Do not claim that competitors are unsafe, unreliable, cheap or inferior unless there is verified evidence.

Instead communicate:

"What should a homeowner check before choosing an elevator?"

This positions Sanyo as an expert and trusted advisor.

---

## 18. Legal and Safety Principle

Safety-related communication must be conservative and accurate.

Never guarantee absolute safety.

Never encourage unsafe installation or operation.

Never provide misleading technical claims.

When discussing regulations, standards or certification:

- Verify the current requirement.
- State the applicable standard clearly.
- Avoid presenting assumptions as facts.

---

## 19. AI Quality Gate

Before publishing any content, check:

- Accuracy
- Brand consistency
- Customer relevance
- Clarity
- Trustworthiness
- Legal/safety sensitivity
- CTA relevance
- Technical accuracy
- No unsupported claims

If any critical item fails:

STOP.

Do not publish.
