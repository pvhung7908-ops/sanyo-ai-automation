# Content Intelligence Boundary

Content intelligence belongs to **System 1**. System 2 only executes an already approved ARTICLE, SOCIAL, IMAGE or VIDEO job.

## Required hand-off

The bridge contract requires an approved objective, audience, platform and verified source references. VIDEO work additionally requires an approved brief and shot plan.

## Execution rule

System 2 may assemble prompts and route production, but it must not invent customer insight, product facts, sales claims or content approval. When the approved input is incomplete, it returns INVALID_CONTRACT or MISSING_REQUIRED_CONTEXT to System 1.

This boundary prevents a duplicate content strategy from accumulating in the automation repository.
