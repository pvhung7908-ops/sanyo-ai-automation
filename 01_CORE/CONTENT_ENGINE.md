# Sanyo Yusoki Vietnam — Content Engine

## 1. Purpose

Content Engine is the strategic content generation system for Sanyo Yusoki Vietnam.

Its purpose is to transform:

Customer problem
→ Customer insight
→ Content angle
→ Story
→ Explanation
→ Solution
→ Trust
→ CTA

The system must prioritize customer understanding before product promotion.

---

## 2. Core Content Principle

Never start with:

"What product do we want to sell?"

Start with:

"What problem is the customer trying to solve?"

The content process is:

Customer
→ Problem
→ Insight
→ Context
→ Education
→ Solution
→ Proof
→ CTA

## 5. Content Angle Selection

After identifying the customer problem, select the strongest content angle.

Possible angles:

### Problem-first

Expose a problem the customer may not have considered.

### Education-first

Teach the customer how to make a better decision.

### Story-first

Tell a realistic family or homeowner story.

### Mistake-first

Explain common mistakes.

### Comparison-first

Compare solutions based on relevant customer criteria.

### Expert-first

Explain the issue from an engineering or architectural perspective.

### Myth-first

Correct a common misunderstanding.

### Checklist-first

Give customers a practical checklist.

### Before-and-after

Show the transformation of a problem into a solution.

## 6. Hook Engine

The hook must create meaningful curiosity.

A good hook should:

- Identify a real problem
- Challenge an assumption
- Reveal a hidden consideration
- Ask a relevant question
- Create useful curiosity

Avoid:

- Fake urgency
- False claims
- Excessive fear
- Misleading clickbait
- Unsupported statistics

Preferred hook patterns:

"Nhà nhỏ có nhất thiết phải bỏ ý tưởng lắp thang máy?"

"Có một vị trí đặt thang máy có thể làm thay đổi cả căn nhà."

"Nhiều gia đình chỉ nghĩ đến thang máy sau khi đã xây xong nhà."

"Điều gì cần kiểm tra trước khi quyết định lắp thang máy cho nhà cải tạo?"

---

## 7. Story Engine

When appropriate, build content around a realistic customer situation.

Story structure:

Situation
→ Problem
→ Discovery
→ Decision
→ Solution
→ Result
→ Lesson

The story should feel realistic and relatable.

Do not invent customer testimonials or project results unless verified.

---

## 8. Insight Engine

Every piece of important content should contain an insight.

The insight should answer:

"Why does this matter?"

Examples:

A small house does not necessarily mean an elevator is impossible.

The elevator position should be considered together with the house layout.

For renovation projects, structural conditions must be examined before selecting an elevator solution.

The cheapest initial option may not always be the best long-term choice.

---

## 9. Explanation Engine

Technical information must be translated into customer language.

Structure:

Technical fact
→ Simple explanation
→ Customer impact

Example:

Technical fact:
Pit depth affects elevator installation requirements.

Customer explanation:
"Độ sâu hố pit ảnh hưởng trực tiếp đến khả năng bố trí thang trong nhà hiện hữu."

Customer impact:
"Vì vậy, nhà cải tạo nên kiểm tra hiện trạng trước khi chốt phương án."

---

## 10. Solution Engine

The solution should answer the customer's actual problem.

Do not force a product into every story.

Possible solutions may include:

- Change elevator position
- Adjust architectural layout
- Optimize cabin dimensions
- Review shaft dimensions
- Evaluate renovation feasibility
- Select an appropriate elevator configuration
- Improve maintenance planning
- Consult technical specialists

Product recommendations should appear only when relevant.

---

## 11. Product Integration

When a Sanyo Yusoki product is relevant:

Feature
→ Functional benefit
→ Customer benefit
→ Context

Never list technical specifications without explaining their relevance.

Do not exaggerate product advantages.

Only use verified technical specifications.

---

## 12. Trust Engine

Trust is created through:

- Accuracy
- Transparency
- Technical competence
- Realistic explanations
- Consistent communication
- Evidence
- Professional service
- Long-term responsibility

Avoid unsupported superlatives such as:

"Best in Vietnam"

"Safest"

"Number one"

"Absolutely safe"

"Cheapest"

unless independently verified and appropriate.

---

## 13. CTA Engine

CTA must correspond to the customer's stage.

### Early stage

Invite education.

Example:

"Anh/chị có thể xem checklist trước khi quyết định."

### Consideration stage

Invite consultation.

Example:

"Anh/chị có thể gửi mặt bằng để kiểm tra sơ bộ vị trí thang."

### Decision stage

Invite action.

Example:

"Đội ngũ kỹ thuật có thể khảo sát hiện trạng và đề xuất phương án phù hợp."

CTA must be:

- Natural
- Useful
- Relevant
- Low pressure

## 18. Content Quality Rules

Before final output, verify:

- Is the customer problem clear?
- Is the insight useful?
- Is the information accurate?
- Is the story believable?
- Is the solution relevant?
- Is the brand consistent?
- Is the language natural?
- Is the CTA appropriate?
- Are technical claims verified?
- Are there unsupported claims?
- Is the content genuinely useful?

If a critical requirement fails:

STOP.

Ask for clarification or verification.

---

## 19. Content Scoring

Score each draft from 1–5:

Customer relevance
Clarity
Insight
Storytelling
Trust
Brand consistency
Technical accuracy
CTA relevance

Minimum recommended publishing score:

4/5 average.

Any safety or factual accuracy issue:

Automatic rejection.
